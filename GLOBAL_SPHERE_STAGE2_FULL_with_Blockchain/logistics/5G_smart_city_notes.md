
Smart City & 5G Notes
---------------------
- 5G layer improves low-latency updates for courier telemetry, drone fleets and IoT sensors in smart warehouses.
- Integrate with telco partners for private 5G slices; use MQTT or WebSocket over low-latency networks for telemetry ingestion.
- Design backend to accept high-frequency telemetry and aggregate into time-series DB (e.g., InfluxDB) for analytics.
